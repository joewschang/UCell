# Same-member Google and LINE login

An existing Web/Google member who opens the OA member center with an unbound LINE account chooses **已用 Google 或 Web 註冊，登入並連結 LINE**. They authenticate using the original registered Google account or member identifier and password. The server authenticates the existing member session and independently verifies the current LIFF ID token before adding a LINE identity to that Person. Registration is not called. Successful linking enters the original member home.

From **會員資料 → 登入方式**, the member can view Google/LINE linked statuses. **驗證並連結我的 LINE** starts LINE login while retaining the current UCell session. After returning to the page, the member explicitly clicks the button again to finish linking. The existing Google-linking flow remains available for members who originally registered through LINE. Only linked statuses are exposed; provider subjects and ID tokens are not displayed or persisted by this feature.

The authenticated member determines the target Person; email, phone number, member number alone, and client-provided LINE userId cannot establish a link. Active repeats are idempotent. A LINE belonging to another Person or a revoked identity cannot be reassigned. A member with another LINE identity cannot replace it through this feature. Concurrent additions are serialized on the Person row, and the provider-subject unique constraint prevents one LINE from belonging to two Persons. Existing company-reviewed recovery remains available through Support. Linking never creates a Person, Qualification, Ball, order, or entitlement.

Stage UAT: register through Google, note member number and existing orders; open the OA center through an unbound LINE; select existing Web member, authenticate with original account, and confirm the same member number/orders. Close and reopen through LINE and then through Google; both should enter the same account. Test wrong password, unregistered Google, already-linked LINE, disabled member, expired LINE token and repeated/concurrent submission. No account transfer or duplicate membership should occur.
